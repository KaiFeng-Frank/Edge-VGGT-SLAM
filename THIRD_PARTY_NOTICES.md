## Co-Me research subset

The [v22 token merge/predictor helper](deploy/rk3588/validation/v22_token_merge.py) and [temporary MLP research wrapper](deploy/rk3588/validation/v22_come_fullmodel.py) adapt the Co-Me authors' implementation at [commit `5c7cfbc14da37f5dbb991e3a6f9e2c4a5efb8833`](https://github.com/co-me-tokens/CoMe/tree/5c7cfbc14da37f5dbb991e3a6f9e2c4a5efb8833). Its attribution and **CC BY-NC 4.0** scope are retained in both helpers; the repository's BSD license does not relicense that adaptation. The author's [pinned root license](https://github.com/co-me-tokens/CoMe/blob/5c7cfbc14da37f5dbb991e3a6f9e2c4a5efb8833/LICENSE) is CC BY-NC 4.0. The released confidence predictor is a separate external checkpoint in that same pinned tree; no predictor-specific license or model card was found beside its checkpoint/config. Its exact source, Git-blob and SHA256 bindings are in [results/come_sources_v22.json](results/come_sources_v22.json). This publication includes neither the predictor checkpoint nor the official source/vendor tree. The original Meta VGGT checkpoint remains subject to its separate terms above; this research does not grant commercial use of either release or establish unchanged-algorithm inference.

No pretrained weights, weight-bearing ONNX/RKNN models, SDK headers or SDK binaries are distributed here. Exporting or compiling a model does not replace the source weights' license with BSD-2-Clause. [UPSTREAMS.json](UPSTREAMS.json) and [results/model_license_v21.json](results/model_license_v21.json) record the inspected revisions and source hashes. License sources were checked on 2026-10-11.

The v31 [dynamic Update research helpers](deploy/rk3588/validation/dpvo_dynamic_update/README.md) retain the same DPVO MIT attribution and adjacent license, including the unchanged v30 lifecycle/encoder copies and topology adaptation. Exported parameters and compiled artifacts stay external; neither source publication nor CPU execution changes the separately documented weight license.
